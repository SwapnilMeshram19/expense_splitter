Privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026. Applies to the Expense Splitter Android app and the website split.ssoftapp.in.

Who we are

Expense Splitter is published under ssoftapp.in. Contact: support@ssoftapp.in.

What we collect and why

  • Your account. When you first open the app, or create or join a group on the website, we make an anonymous account (a random id, no name or email). If you choose to save it, we also keep your email address, or your Google account's email, name and profile picture. Used to sign you in and keep your groups across devices.
  • What you put in groups. Group names, the names of people in them, expenses (description, amount, date, category, notes, who paid and who shares), payments between members, and payment details members choose to add (such as a UPI ID). Receipt photos, if you attach them in the app. Used to calculate balances and show them to the group.
  • Notifications (app). A push token for your phone, so we can notify you about group changes and reminders. You can turn notifications off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Ads (app only). The Android app shows banner ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first. The website has no ads.

We don't use analytics or tracking on the website, and we don't sell your data. Your browser keeps your session and a copy of your groups (local storage and IndexedDB) so pages open quickly and work offline; signing out removes them.

Who can see it

Everything in a group is visible to its members, including people who join later through an invite link. Invite links are valid for 7 days; share them only with people you want in the group.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Brevo: sends the sign-in code emails.
  • Google: Google sign-in, Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications) and AdMob (ads in the app).
  • Cloudflare: hosts this website.

Exchange rates come from public rate services; no personal data is sent to them.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Anonymous accounts that are in no group and haven't been used for 30 days are deleted automatically.

Deleting your data

You can delete expenses and leave groups in the app. To delete your account, email support@ssoftapp.in from the email on the account (or tell us the Google account you use). We delete the account and its personal data within 30 days. Expenses you added to shared groups stay for the other members as part of their records, under the name used in the group.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Each account can only read the groups it belongs to (enforced in the database). Sign-in codes expire quickly and can be used once.

Children

Expense Splitter isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
